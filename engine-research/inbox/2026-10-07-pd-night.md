## 2. Telling night from the log: built (`daynight.c`)

- The sky dome VS (exe, FNV-1a `0x93f9fda4`, already an exe-matrix draw in camlog) carries, at fixed offsets in its
  b0: `KeyLightDirAndXZLength` +80, `TopColourDrk` +96, `HorColourPow` +112, `SunColourPow` +128. The shader dots
  `.xyz` with the dome direction (y up) to the 64th power for the sun glow, so `.xyz` points TOWARDS the key light
  and `.y` is its height `[inferred-static 2026-10-07]`. Hash re-checked against the extracted blob
  `[verified-numerically 2026-10-07, n=1]`.
- New `staging/burnout-paradise-vr/proxy-d3d11-camlog/src/daynight.c`: every 10 s, one log line
  `daynight: DAY|TWILIGHT|NIGHT (sky top luma …), key light dir … (height …), top …, horizon …, sun …`.
  Log only, edits nothing. The DAY/NIGHT words come from uncalibrated thresholds (top luma ≤ 0.05 night,
  ≥ 0.25 day) `[hypothesis]`: the first night run should replace them with measured values. Whether the key light
  turns into the moon at night is unknown `[hypothesis]`. Host test with a stubbed buffer passes (shader filter,
  10 s rate limit, NIGHT word, no read past the buffer) `[verified-numerically 2026-10-07, n=1 test]`.

## 3. Forcing night through constants: not possible as a whole; a narrow switch for the pool only

- Forcing the WORLD to night from the proxy is not realistic: night is spread over many constants (key light
  colour, fog, sky colours, irradiance quadrics, colour cubes in `ENVIRONMENTSETTINGS/COLOURCUBES`) and, more to the
  point, **whether the headlight pool is drawn at all is decided on the CPU**, which we cannot see
  `[inferred-static 2026-10-07]`. Use the menu setting (§1).
- What the pool's strength IS: PS `0x77d46358` outputs alpha = cone texture × min(depth term,
  `g_headlightConstants.x`) × `g_depthConversion.w` `[inferred-static 2026-10-07]`.
- `headlight.c` now (a) logs every 10 s how many pool draws there were and the game's two strength numbers
  (`headlight: N pool draws in the last 10s, …`; "0 pool draws" lines too), which answers "is the pool drawn by day";
  and (b) **numpad 8** (NumLock on) toggles a force: those two numbers are replaced by the largest non-zero values
  seen earlier (a night frame), or 1.0 if none yet. It only helps if the pass is drawn by day with zero strength;
  if the log shows 0 pool draws by day, the force can do nothing and that is the answer `[hypothesis]` until run.
  It composes with the per-eye matrix edit (numpad 5) and puts the game's values back once when both are off.
- Note: the 2026-10-01 logs hold no "first per-eye g_clipToHeadlight upload" line, so with numpad 5 on the
  headlight pass was not seen drawn in those runs (daytime, or numpad 5 not on while it drew) `[inferred-static
  2026-10-07, from burnout_camlog.txt]`.
