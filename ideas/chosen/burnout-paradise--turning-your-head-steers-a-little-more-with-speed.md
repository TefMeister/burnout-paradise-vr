# Turning your head steers a little, more with speed

> "head turning in vr moves the car based on speed, so when driving then moving head left and right turns the
> car, so it feels like a subtle addition to the normal controller steering" — 2026-09-28

While driving, looking left or right in the headset adds a small amount of steering on top of the controller, and
the faster the car goes, the more a head turn counts. The controller stays the main way to steer; the head adds a
natural lean into corners.

**What it'd take:** the headset's head turn (which a VR build reads anyway) mixed into the game's steering input,
scaled by speed. The game's steering is a pad/keyboard input, so the mix could happen where our mod feeds input,
not inside the game's physics. Two things to settle when it is built: a dead zone, so glancing at a mirror does not
steer, and whether it helps or hurts comfort in a driving game. ⚠️ Not checked.
