> "car crashes in burnout paradise switch to 3rd person view"

When the car wrecks, the view leaves the driver's seat and watches the crash from outside, then
returns to the cockpit for the restart. In a headset this also spares the player being thrown
around inside a tumbling car, which is one of the worst things VR can do to a stomach.

**What it'd take:** the game already cuts to its own outside "crash cam" when you wreck, so the
likely route is to let that camera through in VR instead of fighting it — shown as a steady screen
or a fixed spot in space rather than glued to the head. ⚠️ Not checked: we have not yet found how
the game's cameras work.
