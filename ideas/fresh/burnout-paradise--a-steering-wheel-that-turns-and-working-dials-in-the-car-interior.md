> "make turning steering wheels and custom dials into the car interior"

Sitting in the cockpit, the wheel turns with your steering and the gauges (speed, revs, boost) are
real needles that move, instead of a static or missing interior. Cross-reference: this is what
makes a first-person seat feel like a real car in VR.

**What it'd take:** first, finding out what the remaster's cockpit view actually draws — whether
the wheel and dials exist as separate moving parts, or would have to be added as new models by us
and driven from the car's speed and steering. The second is much more work. ⚠️ Not checked.
